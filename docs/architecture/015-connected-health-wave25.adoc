= Wave 25: Connected Component Health

Administering now exposes a metadata-only connected component health report.

== Endpoint

`/admin/connected-components/health.json`

== Responsibility

* Accessing reports authentication/account-administration health without exposing password, session, reset, or second-factor internals.
* Rolling reports ACL administration health without exposing raw subject grants, raw policy internals, secrets, sessions, or passwords.
* Administering aggregates these health checks and uses them as an operator-facing diagnostic surface only.

== Guard

Health checks are not executors. Blocking checks should keep real mutations behind review or disabled until the owning component is ready.
